Privacy

OpenDiag · how your data is handled

On your device

Everything OpenDiag reads (vehicles, reports, logs) stays on your PC or phone. There is no account. You can delete all of it in Settings → Privacy.

What the app sends by itself

Only one thing: when it starts, it asks GitHub which OpenDiag version is the newest, so it can offer an update. Nothing about you or your vehicle is in that request. Turn it off in Settings → Updates.

Sharing results (only if you choose to)

After a scan or hardware test, the app can offer to share an anonymous result so others learn which vehicles and adapters work and which fault codes are common. It asks you once. Every time, it shows exactly what will be sent, and nothing goes until you press Send.

SentThe make, model and year of the vehicle; the first 3 characters of the VIN (the manufacturer code); the adapter, connection type and protocol; the ECUs' calibration IDs; which functions worked; fault codes with their freeze-frame values; readiness monitors; the app version and operating system.
Never sentYour name, the full VIN, your location, notes, files, live data, or anything from the simulator.
StoredThe shared result, the day it arrived and a code that lets you delete it. Your IP address is not stored (a scrambled version is kept for one day to stop abuse, then deleted).
PublishedOnly combined summaries, and only when at least two separate results report the same thing. Individual results are not published.
WhereA Cloudflare Workers service with a D1 database, run by the OpenDiag project (source code).
DeleteSettings → Sharing → Delete removes a shared result from the service at once; Delete all my shared results removes everything you shared from that device.

Questions or requests: open an issue. Full technical details: docs/privacy.md.
